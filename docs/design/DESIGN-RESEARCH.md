## Galleries (Land-book, Awwwards, SaaSFrame, Mobbin, Recent.design)

Not captured: these are aggregators of the sites above. Patterns that recur across them and that we
adopt: two-tone sentence headings, cropped product windows, asymmetric bento with mixed tile sizes,
keycap keyboard hints, mono metadata labels, and section-by-section alternation between dense
directories and spacious showcases.

## Conclusions → NiftyFix principles

1. **The tool is the illustration.** Every visual on the page is a fragment of a NiftyFix screen
   that exists (requirement checks, page grid, size meter). No mascots, blobs or stock art.
2. **Instant access.** Search is the centre of the hero and a ⌘K palette everywhere; it understands
   tool names, aliases, formats ("png to jpg") and size intents ("photo under 50 kb").
3. **Proof over promises.** Pass/warn/not-checked rows, before → after sizes, and the privacy
   verification page replace slogans. No invented metrics, logos or testimonials.
4. **Paper, ink and one signal.** Warm stone ink on cream paper (light) or warm near-black (dark,
   default); the logo orange is the only accent; emerald/amber/red only mean pass/warn/error.
   No blue, purple, violet or pink (owner decision).
5. **Editorial rhythm.** Alternate one spacious showcase with one dense directory; hierarchy comes
   from size (live categories large, planned categories as a compact text index).
