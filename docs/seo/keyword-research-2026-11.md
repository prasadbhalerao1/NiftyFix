## Conclusions

1. **Size-target pages are the Indian long tail.** Each common target (100 KB, 200 KB, 300 KB, 500 KB, 1 MB, 2 MB) is a distinct search with distinct intent; a page with that target preset answers it directly. The big competitors mostly do not have them.
2. **Exam/document intents** (SSC, IBPS, RRB, GDS, PAN, voter ID) are high-value for the Form-ready tools. Per our rule, a portal page needs a verified official specification; collect those first.
3. **Feature gaps signalled by searches:** merge PDF *with images*; page numbers in Roman numerals; PDF to JPG under a KB limit; JPG to PDF under a KB limit.
4. **Titles:** lead with the exact query; include "online free" where it reads naturally; mention the outcome (KB size, A4).
